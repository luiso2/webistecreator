About us

One artist,
thousands of flawless brows

Marlen Diaz founded Lavish Brows in 2021, putting her last savings into an intensive brow certification course. Today she is CEO and Master Artist of her own studio in Pompano Beach, and trains new PMU artists through the Lavish Brows Academy, with in-person and online courses.

The work speaks for itself: nearly 25,000 people follow the studio on Instagram, and clients and students alike say the same thing, a comfortable space, precise results, and a mentor who walks you through every step.
