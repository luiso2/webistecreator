Our story

A little corner of Italy
in Gainesville

Dolce Vita Bakery Café is Gainesville's neighborhood Italian bakery and café: cannoli filled to order, sfogliatelle, rainbow cookies, fruit tarts and fried cronuts made only on Saturdays, all baked fresh every day behind our display case on NW 34th Blvd.

We also make breakfast, Boar's Head deli sandwiches, homemade soups and custom cakes for weddings, baby showers, graduations and events. No booking, no appointments: just walk in, pick what looks good, and enjoy.
