The Studio

One esthetician,
full attention

Kalie Cosgrove has worked with skin for over a decade. Her studio on North Meridian Street runs by appointment only, one client at a time, with no rotating staff and no upselling: just a licensed esthetician who looks at your skin and adjusts the plan in the room.

Instead of a long menu of fixed treatments, Kalie offers facials measured by session length. That gives her room to actually look at your skin, listen to what you want, and build the session around it, combining tools like Gua Sha Fusion and manual lymphatic drainage when they fit.
