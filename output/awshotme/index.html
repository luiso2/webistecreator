About AWSHOTME

One photographer,
fully designed sets

AWSHOTME Photography is run by Adaja White, who moved from Virginia to South Florida to build a studio around one idea: every session gets its own fully designed set, not just a plain backdrop. Clients describe her as personable, patient and full of energy behind the camera.

Sessions range from birthday shoots and family portraits to seasonal mini sessions with elaborate themed backdrops, like the Christmas sets that book out every holiday season. Clients also mention how fast the edited photos come back after the shoot.
