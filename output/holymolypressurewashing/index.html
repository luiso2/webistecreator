One wash,

Holy Moly Pressure Washing is a Clearwater, Florida business founded in 2020. Evan does the work himself: he answers the phone, quotes the job and cleans it, no middlemen and no call center.

He matches the technique to the surface: high pressure for concrete and pavers, and soft washing (low pressure, with solution) for roofs and wood, so nothing gets damaged. That is what his 42 five-star Google reviews describe.
