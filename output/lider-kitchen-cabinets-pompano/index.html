In house fabrication,

Lider Custom Cabinetry is a cabinet shop based in Pompano Beach. We build kitchens, bathroom vanities, closets and entertainment centers to the exact measurements of each home, from the first measurement to the final install.

Several clients on Google have called us back for more than one project in the same home, a closet first, an entertainment wall later. Organized, fairly priced and professional.
