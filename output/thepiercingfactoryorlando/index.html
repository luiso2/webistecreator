The Piercing Factory is a neighborhood studio on S Orange Ave, near Florida Mall. Jenny, a licensed piercer and the studio's owner, works alongside tattoo artists Stitch_in_ink and Ismael, covering everything from a simple lobe piercing to a multi-hour custom tattoo.
