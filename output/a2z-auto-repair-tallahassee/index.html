About A2Z

A family shop
Tallahassee trusts

A2Z Auto Repair is a family-owned shop on W Tharpe St, built on direct communication and real fixes instead of guesswork. Customers come back for the same reason, again and again: when something's wrong with your car, A2Z tells you straight, without the runaround.

The shop works on everything that comes through the bay, Nissans and Jeeps, BMWs and Mercedes-Benz, even the occasional Maserati, each one diagnosed with the same dealer-level scan tools and the same attention.
