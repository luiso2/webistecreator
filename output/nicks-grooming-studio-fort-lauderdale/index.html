About us

A neighborhood studio, built around dogs

Nick's Grooming Studio sits right on Federal Highway in Fort Lauderdale, where owner and groomer Nicolas Curcio and his team look after dogs of every breed and size. Regulars keep coming back for the same reason: a calm hand, real attention to each coat, and a dog that goes home looking, and feeling, like the best version of itself.

It shows in the reviews: Fort Lauderdale families ask for Nick by name, from Havanese siblings to big-energy mixed breeds. As Jeff put it: "Nick and his team love our pups like their own."
