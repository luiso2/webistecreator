About us

Mother and daughter,
one roof

SOHO Wellness & Med Spa began in 2015 when Debbie and her daughter Becky, an esthetician, decided to combine their services under one roof: a restored house in South Tampa, just off Kennedy Blvd, with its own parking lot.

Today the team handles Botox and fillers, facials, massage, infrared sauna and laser across three Tampa Bay locations: Tampa, Wesley Chapel and Westchase, with 101 verified reviews and a 4.8 rating.
