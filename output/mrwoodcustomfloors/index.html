How we work

Your floor, done right the first time

01

Call and quote

You call or message, tell us about the space and the flooring you want, and Andres schedules a visit to measure and quote.

02

On-site measurement

We check the subfloor, moisture and leveling, and show you real hardwood, marble or tile options that fit your budget.

03

Hands-on installation

Andres and his crew install, sand or repair the floor on site, with the same care you would see in a luxury hotel.

04

Walkthrough and warranty

We walk the job together before we leave, and if anything needs a touch-up, Andres comes back with his crew to fix it.
