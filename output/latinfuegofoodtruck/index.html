Nuestra historia

From Peru and China,
to your plate

Latin Fuego is a traditional chifa food truck: the Chinese-Peruvian fusion cuisine born in Lima over a century ago. Lomo saltado, arroz chaufa and stir-fried noodles, wok-cooked over real fire right in front of you.

Their customers confirm it: fresh food, generous portions, and fast, friendly service. A perfect 5.0 across 17 verified Google reviews.
