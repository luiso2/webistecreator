The studio

in Pompano Beach

Hidden Gem Nail Boutique is a private, appointment-only studio at 1 N Ocean Blvd, steps from the ocean. Every visit is one on one: no rush, no waiting room, just your design and Marcela's full attention.

From clean gel manicures to hand painted nail art (Halloween webs, sea turtles, gold marble, candy cane stripes), Marcela's work is what keeps clients coming back. As Amber puts it: "won't go anywhere else".

The process, step by step

How your set comes together

01

Prep & Shape

Cuticle care, filing and the shape you want: almond, stiletto, square or coffin. Marcela shapes every nail to fit your hand.

02

Gel or Dip Foundation

Gel, dip powder, builder gel or Gel X: the right foundation for the durability and strength you want.

03

Custom Nail Art

Hand painted designs, ombre, chrome, marble or gems: if you can describe it, Marcela can paint it.

04

Finish & Seal

High shine top coat, cuticle oil and a final check. You leave with a set built to last for weeks.
