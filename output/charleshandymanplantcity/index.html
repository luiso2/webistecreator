About us

Real repairs,
done right the first time

Charles Handyman Services & More is a Florida LLC based in Plant City. The team covers kitchen and bathroom remodeling, flooring installation, carpentry and outdoor structures, plus the general repairs a home needs over time: drywall, trim and damaged wood.

The philosophy behind the business is simple: the most expensive repairs almost never start out expensive. A small leak turns into mold, a soft spot in the floor turns into a full subfloor job, damaged trim turns into wood rot. Catching it early, backed by a 4.9 rating across 23 Google reviews, is the job.
