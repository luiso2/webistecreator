About us

A family business
that builds around your home

AT Custom Closet is a Central Florida family business dedicated to custom closets and cabinetry. Every project starts by measuring your actual space: nothing off the shelf, everything built for your wall, your corner and your ceiling height.

The same care goes beyond the closet: bathrooms and laundry rooms get the same made-to-measure treatment. It all ends up on Instagram, where 1,462 people follow every finished project.
