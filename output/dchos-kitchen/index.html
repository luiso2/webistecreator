About us

Homestyle food,
right on the street in Seffner

D'chos Kitchen is a family-run food truck in Seffner (Brandon, FL area) serving pupusas revueltas, quesabirria, tacos and Mexican street food. Several customers mention that the owner and his wife cook everything themselves, with the kind of close, personal service you get from a family business.

With 309 reviews and a 4.8 rating on Google, it is one of the highest-rated food trucks in the Brandon area.
