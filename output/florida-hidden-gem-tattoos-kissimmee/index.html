About

a thousand stories in ink

Florida Hidden Gem Tattoos puts real, personal work on skin in the heart of Downtown Kissimmee: fine line florals, bold blackwork, lettering, color pieces and cover-ups, plus piercing, always with new sterilized equipment for every client.

Walk-ins are always welcome, and the studio sits right across from the Toho Square parking garage, so parking is easy and free. Every finished piece gets posted to Instagram, where the portfolio speaks for itself.
