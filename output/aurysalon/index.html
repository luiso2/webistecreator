Our story

A family shop
in the heart of Kissimmee

Aury's Salón & Barber Shop is a family-run shop that has been cutting hair on Pleasant Hill Rd in Kissimmee for decades. Barbershop and unisex salon live under one roof: fades, color, keratin treatments, threading and more, led by Aura and her team.

No rush here: the goal is for you to walk out with the look you asked for. Walk-ins welcome every day.
