The process, step by step

How we build your look

01

Consult

We talk through length, fade and style before the clippers come out, so the cut matches exactly what you have in mind.

02

Lineup & shape

The lineup and shape get marked first, setting the perfect base for the fade or a custom design.

03

Precision fade

A blended transition worked by hand with clipper and scissor, with the same attention paid to the beard if it is part of the visit.

04

Finish

Edges checked, neck cleaned up and one final pass so the cut looks sharp from day one.

The house touch
