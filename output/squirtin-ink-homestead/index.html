The studio

One shop,

Squirtin' Ink is a tattoo and piercing studio in Florida City working in traditional, black and grey, realism, color and script, with artists including Chino and Joel. It runs on walk-ins and virtual consultations, so a first idea can become an appointment the same week.

Reviewers describe it as spotless, well laid out and welcoming, with an atmosphere that makes clients feel like family. That reputation holds up a 4.8 rating across 161 reviews.
