The process, step by step

Here's how your nail appointment flows

01

Consultation & prep

We start by talking through the shape, length and finish you want, followed by filing, cuticle care and nail prep.

02

Color & application

We apply the system you choose, regular, gel, Apres or Luminary, with precision, coat by coat.

03

Nail art & details

French tips, cat eye, chrome, stickers or a custom design, whatever you're after for your set.

04

Hydration & finish

Cuticle oil and a hand or foot massage so you leave with nails, and skin, that are genuinely taken care of.
