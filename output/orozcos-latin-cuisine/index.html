About us

Latin cuisine,
served with heart

Orozco's Latin Cuisine is a full-service caterer that grills live in front of your guests, builds charcuterie and grazing tables, runs the bar, and presents traditional Nicaraguan dishes in clay pots. Everything is designed so the host just gets to enjoy the party.

Anallency, who leads the team, is mentioned again and again in reviews for listening to every detail and letting clients taste the food before confirming the menu.
