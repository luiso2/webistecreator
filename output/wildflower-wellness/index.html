About us

Two specialists,
one micro spa

Wildflower Wellness is the space Alethea, an esthetician, and Cayman Talcott, a massage therapist, built together, operating as Wildflower Wellness LLC. It is a calm, plant-filled treatment room where holistic skincare and bodywork share the same roof.

As a micro spa, they're often with clients, so texting is the fastest way to reach them. Every appointment, from the Signature Bespoke Facial to a 90-minute massage, is booked directly on Vagaro.
