Meet the installer

Charlie Corcoran,
cabinet installer

Centerline Cabinets LLC is Charlie Corcoran, a custom cabinet installer based in Tampa, FL. The shop builds the boxes; Charlie is the one who sets them: plumb, level, scribed to walls that are never straight, with doors and drawers lined up the way they should be.

The work runs from full kitchens to pantries, media units and built-in benches, and it all gets posted: 55 posts on Instagram you can look through before you reach out.
