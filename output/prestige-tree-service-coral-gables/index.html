About us

A crew built for height,
and for emergencies

Prestige Tree Service works on homes across Miami and Miami-Dade County: tree removal, aerial pruning, stump grinding and storm cleanup. Bucket truck, crane or climbing gear, matched to what the job needs, working close to roofs, fences and power lines.

That same gear and those same ropes also answer the call when a cat gets stuck high in a tree or on a roof, day or well past midnight. Showing up when it matters is what holds up the 4.9 rating on Google, backed by 126 real reviews.
