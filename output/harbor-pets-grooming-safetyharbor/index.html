About us

Mary and Kelley,
a trusted duo

Harbor Pets Grooming opened in 2011 in a little blue cottage in downtown Safety Harbor. Mary and Kelley work out of that same shop, bathing, cutting and caring for dogs of every size and coat.

Their Google reviewers say you can tell they love animals the moment you walk through the door, and that dogs with thick, difficult coats come out looking just as good as the easy ones. One customer brought the same dog here for 16 years.
