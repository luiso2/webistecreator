About us

Two owners,
one grooming table

Pelusa's Pet Grooming is Martha and Enrique's shop on West University Avenue, grooming Gainesville's dogs and cats for more than six years. It's a genuinely family-run business: the same two people who answer the phone are the ones holding the clippers.

Clients keep coming back for one reason: real personal attention. Every dog and cat is groomed according to what that specific coat and that specific temperament need, not a fixed script.

How a grooming visit works

01

Call ahead

Call (754) 366-3687 and tell Martha or Enrique what your dog or cat needs. No app, no online calendar, just a real conversation.

02

Drop your pet off at the West University Avenue shop at your scheduled time.
