The process, step by step

This is how your set comes together

01

Consultation & mapping

We talk through your eye shape, your natural lashes and the look you want, from a soft classic to full volume, before a single extension goes on.

02

Prep & isolation

Lashes are cleansed and each natural lash is carefully isolated, for a comfortable, secure base before any application begins.

03

Custom application

Olga hand applies your chosen style, classic, wispy volume, volume or wet lash, with precision and no rush, while you lie back and rest.

04

Finish & aftercare

Every set is checked and evened out, with simple at home care tips and a reminder for your next fill to keep it full.
