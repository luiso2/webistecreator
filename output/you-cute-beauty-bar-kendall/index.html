About us

One space,
made to pamper you

You Cute Beauty Bar was built around one idea: a space for anyone who wants to pamper themselves. Ydalmis Zambrano and the team handle lashes, brows, facials and nails inside a studio designed down to the last detail, at Salons by JC in Town & Country Mall.

The menu runs from lash extensions and brow design to chemical peel and platelet-rich plasma facials, fibroblast pen skin tightening, teeth whitening, manicures and pedicures. And for family visits, there is even a small corner for the little ones.
