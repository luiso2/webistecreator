Nosotros

Comida venezolana,
hecha con cariño de Maracaibo

La Maracucha started as a food truck in Miami in 2017, run by Maria Alejandra Puentes, from Maracaibo, and Eros Puentes. Today they have a permanent spot on University Ave in Gainesville, still cooking arepas, patacones and cachapas from the same recipes.

Everything is made to order, no shortcuts: fresh arepa dough, slow-cooked shredded beef, and the house garlic sauce customers ask for on the side. As one real review puts it: "food is phenomenal and the customer service is outstanding."
