Beauty Beyond Beauty is a women-owned, Latina-owned holistic hair recovery studio inside Suite House at Pines Garden at City Center. Every Hairapy combines a real conversation about your scalp, deep cleansing, therapeutic massage and a quartz singing-bowl sound bath.

Every ritual is led by Juliana, who never rushes an appointment, always explains what she is doing, and turns each visit into a moment that is entirely yours. Her clients describe it as leaving refreshed, beautiful, and grateful.
