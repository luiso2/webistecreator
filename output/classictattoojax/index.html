Our Studio

A Murray Hill fixture
since 2019

Classic Tattoo has been putting bold, traditional work on Jacksonville for years, right here on Edgewood Ave. Walk-ins are always welcome and the shop stays true to old school American traditional: heavy black outlines, saturated color, designs built to age well.

The chair is shared by Sutton, Derek Farrow and Justin Collette, each bringing their own take on the classics, from custom flash sheets to full sleeves.
