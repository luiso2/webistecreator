About me

Photos that feel
completely you

I'm Emily, the photographer behind EmmiClaire Photography. My approach is thoughtful and intentional, resulting in bright, true-to-color images that feel authentic and timeless. I specialize in capturing genuine emotion: real smiles, candid moments, and all the in-between details that make your story unique.

Whether you're planning a wedding, proposal, branding session, or family photos, I'll guide you every step of the way so your photos feel effortless and meaningful. As one client put it: she knew exactly which poses to do.
