La experiencia

A family bakery
built one pie at a time

Sandra's Sweet & Savory is Sandra Manzoni's local, woman-owned business since 2019. Everything is baked in her own Tin City kitchen: the signature Key Lime Pie, specialty pies, blueberry and cranberry-orange scones, oatmeal bars and cookies.

The result: a 4.9 rating across 242 Google reviews, customers who call her dark chocolate-dipped Key Lime Pie the best they have ever tasted, and a chai tea latte that became a local favorite.

Cómo horneamos

From the oven to your table

01

Baked fresh

Every pie starts in Sandra's on-site kitchen at Tin City, made from scratch each morning.

02

Double-thick crust

Her signature Key Lime Pie gets a hand-pressed, double-thick graham cracker crust with real key lime juice, not too sweet or tart.

03

Finished by hand

Some pies get a dark chocolate dip, others come mini as pie pops. Bars, scones and cookies are baked the same way, from scratch.

04

At the shop or the market

Pick it up at the Dockside Boardwalk shop, or catch Sandra at Naples-area farmers markets.
