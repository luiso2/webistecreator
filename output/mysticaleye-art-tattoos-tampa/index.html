The studio

One artist,
your full attention

Mystical Eye is a one-artist studio by design. Pencil Montana works by appointment only, no walk-ins, so every session gets full attention from the first email to the final line. "We will not compromise quality or time spent with clients to take on a higher volume" is not a slogan here, it is how the studio is run.

The studio is Tampa's first 100% vegan-friendly private tattoo space, built around three ideas: art, uniqueness and spirituality. The specialties are illustrative and meaningful: traditional, dot work, Japanese, Polynesian tribal, modern minimal and floral vegan-styled custom pieces, medium to large work that fits the illustrative style Pencil Montana favors.

Your appointment, step by step

How booking works here

01

Email us

Send your idea to mysticaleyestudios@gmail.com with the design, approximate size in inches, body placement, style references and the month you would like your appointment.

02

Review and reply

Being a one-artist studio, not every request can be accepted. Replies usually arrive within about a week.

03

Confirm and deposit

Appointments are booked one to two months out. A $150 drawing deposit confirms your spot.

04

Your session

Come with a valid ID (18+), no companions or children, and no rush: the studio works one piece at a time, never in a hurry.
