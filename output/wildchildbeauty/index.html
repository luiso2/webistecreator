The process, step by step

Inside a lash appointment

01

Consult & Prep

We talk through the look you want, check your natural lashes, and gently cleanse the eye area before we begin.

02

Custom Mapping

Mel maps out your set (classic, hybrid or volume) to the shape of your eye, so the result looks natural on you.

03

Lash by lash, with care for your comfort and your natural lash health, for the best possible retention.
