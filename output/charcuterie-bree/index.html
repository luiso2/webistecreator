About us

One artist,
every board made to order

Charcuterie Bree is a home-based charcuterie studio in Kendall, Miami built around one simple idea: every board should look as good as it tastes. From a single cup to a full grazing table, each order is arranged by hand for the occasion.

Clients keep coming back for birthdays, holidays, baby showers and corporate events, and ask for themed boards built around the occasion, from individual cups for a big group to special seasonal spreads.
