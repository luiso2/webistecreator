About us

Two cultures,
one food truck

Latin Soul Grille is Gary and Maritza Lathion's food truck, 26 years married and 7 years serving food all over Jacksonville. Maritza is Puerto Rican born and cooks her family's recipes; Gary, a Marine veteran, runs the smoker and the Southern-style grill.

The result is literally the best of both worlds: empanadas, jibaritos and rice and gandules alongside slow-smoked pulled pork and chicken. A 4.8 rating across Google, Yelp and RestaurantGuru backs it up.

Our story

How it all started

01

Two cultures, one truck

Maritza is Puerto Rican through and through. When she married Gary, a U.S. Marine veteran, they started combining their two styles of food, and the food truck was born.

02

Maritza's kitchen

Empanadas, jibaritos and rice and gandules made by hand every day. Even the coconut flan follows her mom's original recipe from Puerto Rico.

03

Gary's smoker

Gary served in the Marines from 1989 to 1993, including Desert Shield and Desert Storm. He brings that same discipline to the slow-smoked pulled pork and chicken today.

04

Catering & events

They bring the truck to private parties, offices and events all over Jacksonville. Message on Instagram or call (904) 405-4512 to book.
