About us

One esthetician,
twenty years of craft

Boca Skin Spa is Carla Columbia's rejuvenation and wellness spa on NW 13th St in Boca Raton. With 20+ years of experience, Carla handles every appointment herself: personalized facials, acne treatment, microneedling, dermaplaning, chemical peels and brow design, always with an honest read of what your skin actually needs.

Her clients say the same thing on Google, year after year: some have been booking with Carla for over a decade. They describe her work as the perfect balance of clinical and luxury, expert service that never leaves skin marked up or irritated. A 5.0 rating across 39 reviews on Google.
