El Gallito Bakery bakes Cuban bread, pastelitos and cakes daily from its spot at 1551 SW 27th Ave, in the heart of Little Havana.

Their Google reviewers keep coming back for the same reasons: bread that is fresh every single day, counter service that stays warm even during the holiday rush, and pastelitos locals call some of the best in Miami.
