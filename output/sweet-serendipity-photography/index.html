About us

Over 15 years
documenting real moments

Jaime Swanson is the photographer behind Sweet Serendipity Photography in Gainesville, FL. Before photography became her full-time career, she worked as a Rescue Lieutenant/Firefighter, and that same calm under pressure is what she brings to every wedding and session.

Weddings, engagements, newborns and corporate headshots: every session ends with an online gallery and package options to fit every budget. As one client put it, the result speaks for itself: large gallery with plenty of options.
