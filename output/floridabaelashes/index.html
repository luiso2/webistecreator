Florida Bae Lashes is a lash studio in Riverview, FL, on the southeast side of Tampa. Every set is mapped to your eye and applied lash by lash, from classic and hybrid to volume, mega volume, wet look and anime styles.

Beyond extensions, the studio offers 2 and 3 week fills to keep your set full, plus lash removal, brow tint and waxing, all by appointment.
