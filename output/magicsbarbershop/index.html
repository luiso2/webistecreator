About Us

One shop,
one standard

Magic's Barber Shop is a family barbershop in the heart of Westchester, Miami, open since 2016. The team of barbers handles fades, beard work and hot towel finishes with the same attention to detail in every chair.

It is a shop built for the whole family: clients highlight how well they treat kids and how comfortable the atmosphere feels. Free parking and wheelchair accessible.
