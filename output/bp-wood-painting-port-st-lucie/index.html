El oficio

Painting and trim work,
done the right way

BP Wood & Painting is run by Marconi, who leads a small crew alongside Carlos on interior and exterior jobs across Port St. Lucie. Clients consistently point to the same things: a fast, accurate estimate, work that starts and finishes on time, and the kind of attention to detail that shows in the corners other crews rush through.

The result: a 4.9 rating across 45 Google reviews, with finish carpentry working right alongside the paint crew: drywall repair, crown molding, baseboard installation and color consultation, so one crew can carry a room from bare wall to finished.
