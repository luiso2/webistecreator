The experience

A calm groomer,
right in your driveway

Celeste has more than 30 years of experience working with pets. Her fully equipped grooming trailer pulls right up to your home in Gainesville, no cages and no waiting room: your pet goes straight from your yard to the grooming table.

She's known for her patience with anxious, senior or difficult dogs, something her own clients repeat in review after review. As her own listing puts it: "I provide only mobile services", so spa day stays simple for you and calm for your pet.

How it works

Here's a day with Celeste

01

Book on Booksy

Pick the package for your pet's size and coat and book online. Celeste confirms an arrival window for the trailer at your address.

02

Arrival & calm greeting

The fully equipped trailer parks right at your door. Your pet steps straight in, no cage and no waiting room, at an unhurried pace.

03

Bath, cut & care

Bathing, deshedding or a full cut based on the chosen package, with gentle handling built for anxious, senior or difficult dogs.

04

Finishing touches

Nails, ears and a bow or bandana to finish. Your pet heads back inside camera-ready, without ever leaving your street.
