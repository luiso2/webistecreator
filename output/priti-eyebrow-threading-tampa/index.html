The experience

A private studio,
perfected by hand

Priti Eyebrow Threading and Waxing lives inside Sola Salon Studios in New Tampa / Wesley Chapel: a private, single-specialist suite, not an open salon floor. That is where Priti practices Indian eyebrow threading, a cotton-thread technique that shapes the brow hair by hair, with no chemicals and no irritation.

New clients in the area find her and stay: reviews describe expert hands, consistency visit after visit, and referrals between friends. Beyond threading, the menu adds full body waxing, facials and henna design.

The method, step by step

This is how threading works

01

Consult & shape mapping

Priti reviews your natural growth pattern and symmetry before touching a single hair, mapping the shape that suits your face.

02

Precision threading

The Indian cotton-thread technique lifts hair out at the root, strand by strand, with no chemicals and none of the skin contact that usually causes irritation.

03

Tint & finish

On request, eyebrow tinting to fill and define, plus a fine cleanup pass on any stray hairs around the final shape.

04

Aftercare & you're done

Quick aftercare tips and that's it: you leave with the brow done, without the lingering redness other methods leave behind.
