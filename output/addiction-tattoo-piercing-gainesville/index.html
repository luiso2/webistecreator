The shop

One shop,
many styles

Addiction Tattoo & Piercing is a family-run studio just off W. University Ave in Gainesville, tucked behind Enterprise Rent-A-Car. The artists cover a wide range: Marc's Disney, anime and comic book work, Keith's versatility across styles, Tony's freehand mandalas, and John Schmitt's custom pieces.

Clients keep coming back for the same reasons: professional, welcoming service and quality work that lasts, plus a piercing team that takes the time to make first-timers comfortable. A 4.8 rating across 303 reviews on Google and Facebook says the rest.
