The bakery

Family owned,
baked fresh daily

Tony's Bakery is a family-owned Middle Eastern bakery and grocery market tucked into an unassuming industrial stretch near Semoran Boulevard in Orlando. For more than 20 years, the ovens have turned out fresh pita bread every single day, with a whole wheat batch baked every Wednesday.

Alongside the bakery case, shelves of Middle Eastern and Mediterranean grocery staples fill the market, and on weekends the kitchen turns out Persian hot food and rice platters that locals call ahead to reserve.
