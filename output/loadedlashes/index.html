01 · The Studio

An intimate lash studio in St. Petersburg

Loaded Lashes is the studio of L'oreal, a licensed Full Specialist esthetician who specializes in lash extensions. Every appointment starts with a consultation and mapping of your eye or brow, then the set is applied by hand, lash by lash, and never rushed.

Beyond lashes, Loaded Lashes offers microshaded ombre brows, brow lamination and waxing, and trains new lash artists through its own Classic Lash Extension Training, which is why every set carries that level of technical detail.

02 · The Process

How your set is built

01

Consult & mapping

Come with a clean canvas, lashes free of mascara. We map your eye or brow shape and choose the style, curl and thickness before starting.

02

Prep

The eye and brow area is cleansed and protected so the application is comfortable, hygienic and holds with the best retention.

03

Application

Applied lash by lash (Classic, Volume, Mega Volume or Wet Set), or microshaded ombre brows, with unrushed, careful precision.

04

Finish & aftercare

Symmetry check, final styling and aftercare tips so your set lasts. Not happy within 24 hours? A free removal is included.
