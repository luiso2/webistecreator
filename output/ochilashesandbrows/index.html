OCHI Lashes and Brows is Nelia Ochi's private studio in Gulfport, in the St. Petersburg metro area. Every appointment is one-on-one: no shared chairs, no rush, just your eyes or your brows and her hands.

Clients describe the studio as soothing, almost meditative, and Nelia as a perfectionist who explains every step, from custom Brow Architecture mapping to lash lifts and full lash sets. It shows: a perfect 5.0 rating across 20 Google reviews.
