The process, step by step

Here is how your visit unfolds

01

Style consult

We go over reference photos together and pick the look: classic, hybrid, wet set, volume or wispy, based on the effect you want.

02

Prep & hygiene

Natural lash cleansing, mapping and careful isolation, with disposable tools and disinfection between clients.

03

Application with check-ins

Dani applies extension by extension and pauses to confirm with you that the style and curl are going the way you wanted.

04

Final polish

Lash brushing, symmetry check and aftercare tips. You leave ready for your event, your trip, or just because.
