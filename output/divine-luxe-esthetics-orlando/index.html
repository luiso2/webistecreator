The studio

Divine Luxe Esthetics is licensed lash artist Bekah Romero's studio, based in Orlando, FL since 2022. Bekah specializes in wispy looks, customized sets and lash mapping tailored to each client's eye shape, whether it is your first set or you have worn extensions for years.

Her clients say the same thing on Google: a clean, relaxing and professional space from the moment you walk in, and a natural yet full result that clearly comes from someone who cares about her work.
