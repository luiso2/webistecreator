About us

A little Cuban window,
well known in Brandon

La Cocinita Cubana is a neighborhood Cuban counter on Central Dr: a small building with a striped awning, a palapa umbrella and red picnic tables outside. From that little window comes a short, honest menu of pressed sandwiches, pan con lechon, croquetas, pastelitos and cafecito.

No pretense, no giant menu: just real Cuban food, made by hand, in a place its customers keep describing the exact same way: "hidden gem."

Why people keep coming back

It's all in the reviews

01

The real Cuban sandwich

Ham, roast pork and swiss, pressed the way it should be. Customers call it "the real deal" sandwich, and it shows in the name.

02

Slow-roasted pork, daily

Pan con lechon comes from slow-roasted pork with mojo and onion. Reviewers name it among the best Cuban food in the Tampa Bay area.

03

Everything made by hand

No chain, no shortcuts: everything comes out of one small kitchen. As one customer put it, "I don't know how she does it, but the food that comes out of this tiny kitchen is excellent."

04

A loyal following for years

Regulars say they've been stopping in since 2016, and the food and service "has always been spectacular."
