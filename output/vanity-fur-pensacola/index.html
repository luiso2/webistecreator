About

One team,
trusted for years

Vanity Fur is a dog grooming salon in Pensacola led by Donna and her team of groomers. The menu covers baths, breed cuts, nail trims and ear cleaning, with paw treatment and teeth cleaning add-ons. The team is known for its patience with senior dogs, puppies, fearful dogs, large breeds and dogs that need grooming rehabilitation.

With a 4.8 rating from more than 169 Google reviews and voted winner of Best Dog Groomer in Inweekly's Best of the Coast 2025, families across Pensacola have trusted Vanity Fur for years, with one client bringing all 7 of her dogs there for the past 8.
