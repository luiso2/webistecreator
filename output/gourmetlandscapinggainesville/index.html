About us

Nearly twenty years,
one focus

Gourmet Landscaping Services is a Gainesville company founded in 2007. We specialize in low voltage landscape lighting: design and installation with zoning, dimming and color options, plus landscape design, artificial turf and putting greens.

We do not improvise with the lighting on your property: every project is designed around your terrain and existing plantings, and installed to last. With a 4.8 rating on Google from 30 reviews, the work speaks for itself.
