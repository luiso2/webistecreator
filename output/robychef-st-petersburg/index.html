Meet the chef

Chef Roberta,
from Bologna to Tampa Bay

Roberta trained at the International School of Italian Cuisine (ALMA) in Parma and has more than ten years of hospitality experience across Europe and the US. She moved to St. Petersburg in 2015 and now cooks private dinners and event catering built around traditional regional Italian recipes, right in the client's own home.

Clients keep mentioning the same thing: tableside presentations, like her signature flaming cheese-wheel pasta, and a warm, attentive service from start to finish.
