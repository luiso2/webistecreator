About Us

Real Cuban food,
one truck at a time

Tito's Latin Cuisine is a family owned and operated food truck serving Cuban and Latin dishes across Central Florida. From empanadas to mojo pork to loaded yuca fries, everything is made to order and served fast, wherever the truck sets up next.

You will find us parked at regular spots like Lakeland Regional Hospital, Swan Brewing and Groove Roots Brewery in Winter Haven, plus farmers markets and private events. We also cater parties and larger gatherings: the same food, brought to you.
