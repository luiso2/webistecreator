About

A food truck
that earns its line

Al's Barn works out of 3410 S Kings Ave in Brandon, serving scratch-made American comfort food: build-your-own smash burgers with Al's awesome sauce, wings in a long list of flavors, loaded fries and tots, and a poutine customers compare to the real thing in Canada.

163 reviews and a 4.8 rating on Google tell the same story: fresh ingredients, generous portions, and a menu that mixes up flavors so regulars always find something new to try.
