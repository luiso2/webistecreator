Our team

One team,
your whole family

Tampa Bay Family Clinic is a family medicine practice in Tampa. Dr. Mario J. Gonzalez and Dr. Yaraymi Garcia lead a team of doctors, nurses and physician assistants who work together to give you the care you need, at a time that fits your day.

Our office is at 7206 N Armenia Ave, and we work in both English and Spanish. We accept Medicare, Medicaid, Aetna, Cigna, Florida Blue, BlueCross BlueShield, Humana, CarePlus and uninsured patients.

Your visit, step by step

How it works at our office

01

Call or message

Call (813) 933-2880 or email us to book your visit or check availability.

02

Bring ID and insurance

At the front desk we check your ID and insurance, or your payment option if you are uninsured.

03

See the doctor

Dr. Gonzalez or Dr. Garcia sees you, reviews your case, and orders any lab work or exam you need.

04

Follow your plan

You leave with your diagnosis, prescription or results explained, and your next appointment booked if needed.
