How a visit works

01

Free consultation

Tuesday and Wednesday, your hair's condition is reviewed and the process is mapped out before booking any new service.

Shampoo, conditioner and, when your hair calls for it, a loc detox to get the scalp ready for the process.

Technique, your way

Starter locs, micro loc maintenance, wicks or protective braids, each technique worked with real time for the detail.

You leave with your style finished, whether that's an updo, a knot style or the simple look you chose, ready to show off.
