How it works at InkPacto

01

Consult & design

Share your idea on Instagram (@mansotattoo) or at the studio: size, style and placement shape the design before booking.

02

Hygiene first

The space is prepped and sanitized before every session, with single-use materials: cleanliness is the studio's top priority.

03

The session

From 1-hour sessions to 8-hour blocks for large full color or black & gray pieces, with full dedicated time for your design.

04

Aftercare

You leave with aftercare instructions and, if your piece needs more than one session, the next appointment is booked.
