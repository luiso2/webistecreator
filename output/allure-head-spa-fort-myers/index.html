Your visit, step by step

The head spa ritual

01

Consultation

Before we begin, your esthetician checks your scalp and skin to choose the right treatment and pressure for you.

02

Deep cleanse

Steam and professional products open the follicles and lift away product buildup, oil and dead skin from the scalp.

03

Scalp massage

The heart of the session: a slow, technique-led scalp massage that extends into the neck and shoulders to release tension.

04

Nourish

We finish with a nourishing hair mask or treatment and guidance to keep your scalp healthy between visits.
