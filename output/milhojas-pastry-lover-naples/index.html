About

Colombian desserts,
made with love

Milhojas Pastry Lover travels around Naples in its pink trailer serving classic Colombian desserts: puff pastry milhoja with cream, traditional oblea, cholado and merengon, loaded with fresh fruit.

Most of the ingredients are homemade: the owner makes the dulce de leche and the meringues herself, and it shows in every 5-star review.
