The process, step by step

Here is how your visit unfolds

01

Consult & design

A short talk on lash curl, length and brow shape before starting, matching the technique to your eye shape and lifestyle.

02

Prep & isolation

Careful cleansing and lash-by-lash isolation (or clean mapping for microblading and lamination), so the result is precise and long lasting.

03

Application

Classic, hybrid or volume fans applied lash by lash, or pigment and lamination solution worked in with a steady hand.

04

Finish & aftercare

A final check for symmetry and retention, plus aftercare tips so your set or brows last as long as possible.
