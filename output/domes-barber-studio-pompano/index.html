Domes Barber Studio opened in 2022 at 1 N Ocean Blvd, Suite 101, Studio #20, in Pompano Beach: a private, single-chair studio run by Dom. No rotating barbers, no waiting room, every appointment is yours alone.

Clients keep coming back for the same reason: a perfect 5.0 rating across 85 reviews on Booksy, and a barber who takes his time on every fade, every beard and every hot towel shave.
