About us

One groomer,
zero stress for your dog

Barking Lot Mobile Pet Grooming is a mobile grooming van based in Clearwater, FL, run by Edina. Instead of a car ride and a salon waiting room, the fully equipped van pulls up to your driveway for a bath, haircut and care, all in one visit.

Edina has been grooming local dogs for more than 10 years, including clients with mobility issues who need extra patience and care. Regulars, like golden retriever Nemo, know the sound of the van and wait by the window for her to arrive.
