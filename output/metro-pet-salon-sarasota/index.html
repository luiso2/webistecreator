Our Story

A Sarasota fixture,
built on trust

Metro Pet Salon is a real storefront in the heart of downtown Sarasota, led by Amanda and her team. All breeds are welcome, with a specialty in poodles, and the kind of gentle, patient handling that small and toy breeds need most.

Clients have trusted Amanda for over a decade, some for more than 13 years. The same word keeps showing up in their reviews: caring.
