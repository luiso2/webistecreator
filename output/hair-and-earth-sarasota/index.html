About us

One stylist,
organic roots

Hair & Earth is owned and run by Erinn Earth, a certified yoga teacher with advanced massage training and 15+ years of experience in the Sarasota area. Before hair, she worked as a social worker with a psychology degree, and that care for people shows in every appointment.

Every color, cut and scalp treatment uses safe, organic products, and the signature Ayurvedic Scalp Massage adds intentional breathing, warm oil and pressure-point massage across the scalp, neck, jaw, face and ears, plus red light therapy and hot steam towels.
