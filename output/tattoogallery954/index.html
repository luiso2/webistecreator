The shop

A Fort Lauderdale Beach corner that has been inking since 1998

The Tattoo Gallery is a walk-in studio right on South Fort Lauderdale Beach Blvd: black walls, a skull and butterfly mural at the entrance, piercing jewelry cases, and work chairs in plain view. Tourists and locals wander in off the beach and walk out with something new on their skin.

Customers mention the same artists over and over in their reviews, Sergio, Nicole and Rachel among them. Every piece, from the smallest to the largest, gets the same patient attention.
