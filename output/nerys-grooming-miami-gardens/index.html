About Nery's

Neighborhood grooming,
with an eye for detail

At Nery's Grooming every pet gets personal attention: from a basic bath and trim to a clean breed-standard cut or a creative color job, tailored to your dog's coat and temperament. The salon also doubles as a retail shop for grooming supplies, for anyone who wants to keep their pet looking good between visits.

That is what backs up a 5.0 rating across 31 reviews: a neighborhood salon in Miami Gardens (Carol City) where your dog is never just another slot on the schedule.
