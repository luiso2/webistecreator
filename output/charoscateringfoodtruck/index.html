The experience

Charo’s Catering,
Cuban &amp; Venezuelan flavor

Charo’s is a food truck with its own patio on NW 127th Ct, Ocala: wooden tables, umbrellas and music while every plate is cooked to order. The menu mixes milanesas, Cuban sandwiches and tacos with the family’s homemade touch.

The result: a 5.0-star rating across 36 Google reviews, with customers who drive out of their way just to come back.
