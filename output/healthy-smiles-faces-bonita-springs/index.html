About us

One small studio,
personalized care

Healthy Smiles & Faces is Jenny's facial aesthetics studio, tucked inside a small beauty plaza on Bonita Beach Rd in Bonita Springs. She works with PDRN treatments, PRP-style protocols and LED light therapy, always walking clients through the treatment before it starts.

Her clients describe the same thing review after review: a warm, attentive approach, a clean and relaxing space, and results that show up in the skin. A 5.0 rating across 21 reviews online, with clients who have been coming back for over a year.
