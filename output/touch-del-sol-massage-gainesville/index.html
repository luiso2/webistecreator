About Touch del Sol

Natasha,
and nobody else

Touch del Sol is Natasha's massage and bodywork studio inside Sixth Street Wellness Center in Gainesville. She works with Thai massage, craniosacral biodynamic therapy and deep, intentional bodywork, matching her pressure and approach to what each body actually needs.

Her clients keep coming back for years: Jaclyn has been seeing her for over five years and still calls every session 'a positive and healing experience.' Touch del Sol is women-owned and Latino-owned, identifies as LGBTQ+ owned, and is LGBTQ+ friendly.
