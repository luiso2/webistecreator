The studio

A creative home
in Doral

Ink For The Soul is a Doral tattoo studio built around craft, not volume. Owner Johan Avila leads a small team of resident artists, each with their own specialty, from black and grey realism to delicate fine line and bold color pieces.

Clients often describe the shop as relaxed and welcoming: the kind of place where a consult turns into a conversation, and a first visit into something recurring.
