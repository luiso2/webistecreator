Our story

A line that
is worth it

Green's Bakery & Sandwich Shop is a small grab-and-go soul food counter and bakery case on the south side of St. Petersburg. Home-cooked plates, sandwiches, and a case full of pies, peach cobbler, pound cakes and fresh donuts.

They open Thursdays only, and regulars will tell you: the line outside gets long, but the food backs it up. Cash only, so come with bills.
