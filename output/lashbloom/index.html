The process, step by step

This is how your set comes together

01

Consultation & design

We talk through your eye shape and the look you want, from a soft natural set to full glam, before any lash goes on.

02

Isolation & application

Each natural lash is carefully isolated and a classic, hybrid or volume extension is applied by hand for a comfortable, long lasting bond.

03

Final style

Every lash is checked, shaped and blended into a finished curve, so the set looks full and even from every angle.

04

At home care

You leave with simple aftercare tips to protect your set, plus a reminder to book your 2 to 3 week fill and keep your lashes full.
