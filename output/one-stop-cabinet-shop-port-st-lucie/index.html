One Stop Cabinet Shop is a family business run by Ray, serving Port St. Lucie and South Florida. The shop builds kitchen cabinets, does full cabinet refacing, and makes custom closets and built-ins.

Clients point to the careful design and the attention to detail at every stage, from the first quote to the final installation.
