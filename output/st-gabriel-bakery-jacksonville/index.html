Our Story

One family,
one artisan oven

St. Gabriel Bakery opened in March 2024 in Jacksonville's Southside, founded by Carol Vela, her husband Kevin Suarez, and their kids. Every loaf, pastry and sandwich is made from scratch, the same way the family has always done it.

The oven turns out 40 to 45 varieties of Guatemalan and international bread every day, alongside sweet pastries, sandwiches and flatbreads made to order. It is a small, unassuming storefront with a big following in the neighborhood.
