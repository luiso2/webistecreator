The studio

Precision, refined one nail at a time

Nails.room.Clearwater is a boutique Russian manicure studio inside Phenix Salon Suites, on Countryside Blvd in Clearwater. Every set is built with hard gel or rubber base, structured for a sharp, long-lasting finish, from a classic no-polish manicure to a full nail extension.

Behind every appointment is Viktoria Kravets, a certified nail artist with 6+ years of experience trained in Europe, elite-trained in advanced techniques and specialized in sharp precision, long-lasting structure and extreme designs. Expect flawless results, every time.

The process, step by step

This is how your Russian manicure is built

01

Cuticle preparation

Russian (e-file) technique: the cuticle is worked dry, with precision, no soaking, for a clean contour and a flawless base.

02

Shape & structure

Every nail is filed to the chosen shape and reinforced with hard gel or rubber base for a lasting structure that holds up to daily wear.

03

Color & design

Gel color, ombre, chrome, cat eye or French: the design is applied layer by layer and cured under a UV lamp for a high-gloss finish.

04

Flawless finish

Sealing, cuticle oil and one final check on shine and shape. You leave with a sharp, even set built to last.
