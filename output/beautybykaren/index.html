Your visit, step by step

Here's what to expect with Karen

01

Consultation & design

We talk through the shape, look and result you want. Karen maps out the brow design or lash style before any work begins.

02

Prep

Cleansing, and numbing if the service calls for it (lip blush, ombre brows). Everything is set up clean for each client.

03

Application

Lash-by-lash placement or hand-applied pigment work, with precise technique and quality products for ombre brows, lip blush or extensions.

04

Aftercare

You leave with clear aftercare instructions and product recommendations so your results last longer.
