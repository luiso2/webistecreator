About Pawsome Luxuries

One mobile groomer,
zero stress for your dog

Pawsome Luxuries is a mobile grooming service based in Tallahassee, run by Kate. Instead of loading your dog into the car and waiting in a salon, the full bath-and-haircut setup pulls right up to your driveway, all in one visit.

Every dog gets one-on-one attention, no crates and no other dogs barking in the background. With 80 reviews and a 4.9 rating on Google, even dogs that normally hate going to the groomer come out relaxed.
