Our truck

One family,
real home cooking

Mimi's Meals is a Cuban and Latin American food truck based in Okeechobee, FL. The red trailer serves homemade empanadas, Cuban sandwiches, picanha steak and Mimi's signature loaded fries, all cooked to order and served at the picnic table next to the truck.

Travelers passing through Okeechobee for work keep finding it by word of mouth: a perfect 5.0 across 47 verified Google reviews, with regulars praising the portions and the owners' warmth.
