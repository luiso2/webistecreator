The method, step by step

How every set is built to last

01

Consult & shape

We talk through the shape, length and style you want before a single nail is touched, so the result matches what you asked for.

02

Prep & foundation

Cuticle work, filing and a soak off of any old product if needed. Nicole does not work over old acrylic: a clean nail bed is what makes everything else hold.

03

Sculpt & art

Nicole's own hand built acrylic system goes on, followed by hand painted art, stones, encapsulated designs or ombre, whatever you picked.

04

Seal & finish

A sealing top coat, final shaping and cuticle oil, so you walk out with a glossy finish that holds up.
