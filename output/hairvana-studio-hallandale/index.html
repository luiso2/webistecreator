Our story

A Dominican salon
in Hallandale Beach

Hairvana Studio (also known as Hairvana Beauty Studio) is a Dominican-owned hair salon and spa on Hallandale Beach Blvd. Stylist Julissa Tejeda leads the team alongside Marisol and Johana, in a warm, eclectic space with plants and mirrors that make the salon feel welcoming.

Full hair color, balayage and treatments like keratin and hair botox sit alongside blowouts, beach waves and extensions. Clients say "the team is fun and attentive" and that the stylists "are so professional and ethical."

The process, step by step

Here is how your visit unfolds

01

Consultation

A conversation about your hair history, the color or cut you want and the time you have available, before we start.

02

Dominican wash

Wash and prep in the classic Dominican style, with professional products that leave hair ready for color or treatment.

03

Color, cut or treatment

Full hair color, balayage, keratin, hair botox or the cut you chose, applied with precision by Julissa, Marisol or Johana.

04

Final style

Blow dry, beach waves or the style you prefer to walk out camera ready, with tips to maintain the result at home.
