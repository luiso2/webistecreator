The Experience

A quiet luxury ritual room

Serenity Head Spa is a Japanese-technique head spa tucked into Hunters Creek, Orlando: warm marble tones, gold hardware and candlelight built for one purpose, deep relaxation for scalp and mind. Every ritual layers scalp hydromassage, jade stone work, hot towels and aromatherapy.

Go solo or bring someone: the double head spa with jade stones is one of the most requested rituals, and every session closes the way their own promise reads: relax, renew, reborn.

The Ritual, Step by Step

This is how a head spa feels

01

Diagnosis & Cleanse

Your ritual opens with a calming scalp hydromassage under the Japanese-technique halo rinse, cleansing deeply while easing tension from the first minute.
