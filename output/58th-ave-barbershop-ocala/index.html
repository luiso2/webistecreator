About us

One barbershop,
trusted since 2012

58th Ave Barber Shop is a family barbershop on SE 58th Ave in Ocala. Men's cuts, women's cuts and kids cuts, all starting at $12, cash only: the same straightforward service since we opened our doors in 2012.

No appointment, no fuss: you walk in, take a seat and get a clean, honest haircut. It is the kind of neighborhood shop regulars have kept coming back to for over a decade, reflected in a 4.8 rating from 92 reviews on Google.
