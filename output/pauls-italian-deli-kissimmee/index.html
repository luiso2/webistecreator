About us

Three generations,
one deli

Paul's Italian Deli & Restaurant is a family-run Italian deli in Kissimmee making New York style subs and sandwiches with Boar's Head meats. Beyond the counter, we also put together catering trays for offices, meetings and private events.

With over 1,200 reviews on Google, customers say the same thing again and again: it feels like home, the service is fast and friendly, and three generations of the same family still work behind the counter.
