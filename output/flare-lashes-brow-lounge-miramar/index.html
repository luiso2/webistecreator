About us

A private lash suite,
one set at a time

Flare Lashes & Brow Lounge is run by Camellia, a certified lash artist, out of a private studio on North Sherman Circle in Miramar, FL. Every appointment is one-on-one: classic, hybrid and volume lash extensions, from a natural everyday look to the wispy, strip-lash-inspired sets the studio is known for, plus the Detox Ritual and Smooth Me Out facials.

Clients keep saying the same thing on Fresha: a relaxing chair, natural-looking results and lashes that hold up. Flare is a woman-owned, Black-owned studio that works only with vegan lash products. A 5.0 rating across 201 reviews on Fresha.
