About us

One artist,
bronzed results

Drea is a Certified Master Lash and Brow technician. Originally from San Francisco, she moved to Miami in 2022 to set her roots in the Brickell beauty industry. She started her career as lead esthetician at Dashbar before venturing out on her own with Beauty and Bronze.

Her specialties are lashes, brows, full body sugaring and spray tanning. The work speaks for itself: a 5.0 rating across 210 verified reviews on Fresha, with clients who keep coming back visit after visit.
