Our Story

One family,
one cake at a time

Dominican Cakes & Bridal Shop is a family business run by Juan & Eva, LLC on State Road 7, right where Pompano Beach meets Margate. Every cake starts as a conversation: the flavor, the occasion, the design, and it comes out of the kitchen built around exactly what you asked for.

Reviewers keep coming back to the same flavors: dulce de leche, guava, pineapple, the classic Dominican bizcocho taste, moist and not overly sweet. Alongside the cakes, the shop also carries bridal accessories, so brides can stop by for both in one visit.
