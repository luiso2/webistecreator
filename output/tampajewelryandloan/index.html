Our Story

A family business,
built on trust

As a family-owned and operated pawn shop, Tampa Jewelry & Loan has been our home for decades. Centered in the heart of this growing city, we're Tampa natives, highly respected and trusted for buying and selling the highest quality jewelry, watches and luxury goods.

We have a genuine passion for appraising and maintaining pristine condition in every piece that comes into our store, and every item is genuine and backed by our commitment to quality. Even if you have a watch or jewelry repair, we have experts on hand that can restore your item back into perfect working condition.
