About us

One detailer,
obsessed with detail

Melvin Auto Detailing LLC is a mobile detailing service based in Kissimmee, FL. Melvin himself shows up to every appointment, looks over the vehicle and does the work: no subcontractors, no middlemen.

Clients on Google describe him as meticulous and professional, someone who pays attention to every detail and gives honest recommendations without pushing extra services. With 153 reviews and a perfect 5.0 rating, he is one of the highest rated detailers in the Kissimmee and Orlando area.
