The protocol, step by step

Inside an Integrative Therapeutic session

01

Assessment & Intention

Every session starts with a short conversation about your body, your pain points and what you need today, so the protocol is built around you.

02

Deep Tissue & Myofascial Work

Deep tissue massage, myofascial release and trigger point work focus on the areas that need it most: back, legs, glutes, abdomen, arms and neck.

03

Hot Stones & Lymphatic Flow

Manual lymphatic drainage and hot stone therapy, included in every session at no extra cost, help ease tension and deepen the relaxation.

04

Guided Somatic Meditation

Each protocol closes with a guided somatic meditation with binaural audio, to calm the nervous system, reduce stress and help the results last longer.
