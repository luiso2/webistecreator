About Us

Drywall repair,
done right the first time

Can Do Home Improvements is a Cape Coral, FL contractor specializing in drywall repair, texture matching and painting. Led by Paul Auger, a drywall expert since 1976, the crew handles everything from small ceiling patches to full-room repairs after plumbing or storm damage.

The business is veteran-owned and has served Lee County since 2012. On Google and HomeAdvisor, customers point to the same things again and again: fair pricing, careful work, and jobs finished on schedule.

How We Work

From first call to finished wall

01

Call or Text

Tell us what's damaged: a ceiling stain, a hole in the wall, a room that needs texture matched. A few quick questions and we'll know what to bring.

02

Free On-Site Estimate

We come out, look at the damage in person, and give you a straightforward price before any work starts. No surprises.

03

Repair & Texture Match

We cut out and replace the damaged drywall, then match the existing texture so the repair blends into the wall or ceiling around it.

04

Clean Finish & Walkthrough

We sand, prime and leave the area ready for paint, or paint it if that's part of the job, and clean up before we go.
