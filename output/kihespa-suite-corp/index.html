The studio

A private retreat
in the heart of Little Havana

Kihespa Suite Corp is a massage studio at 2727 SW 6th St, Miami, with its own parking and close, attentive service. If you would rather not move, the team brings the portable table to your home or hotel instead: the same therapeutic massage, wherever you are.

Enrique, the lead therapist, is the name that keeps coming up in the Google reviews. Women-owned business, LGBTQ+ friendly, open 7 days a week from 9 AM to 9 PM.
