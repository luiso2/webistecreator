About

One baker,
obsessed with detail

Patti started making custom cakes in 2018 and has baked every order herself from her kitchen in Brandon ever since. She combined her love of baking with her artistic side, and today PattiCakes Custom Cakes LLC is a member of the Valrico/FishHawk Chamber of Commerce.

Birthday, wedding, baby shower and graduation cakes, cupcakes by the dozen, hand-decorated cookies and cake pops: every order is designed around whatever you're celebrating.
