About us

One electrologist,
twenty-five years of craft

GIVE Skin Care is Katrina Deem's medical aesthetic studio in Estero, FL, supervised by medical director Dr. Jerilee Lomas, DO. Katrina is a Certified Clinical and Medical Electrologist with training in Class IV laser, light-based energy, microneedling, radiofrequency and HydraFacial, built over more than a decade working alongside doctors and surgeons across Lee and Collier County.

Every visit starts with an honest read of what your skin needs, not a menu of upsells. Clients on Google describe her as the ultimate professional, knowledgeable and solutions-oriented, some coming back for skincare for over five years. A 5.0 rating across 14 reviews on Google.
