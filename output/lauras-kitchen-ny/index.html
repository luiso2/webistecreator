Our Story

Boricua & Italian flavor
with New York soul

Laura's Kitchen Taste of New York is a food truck in Brandon, Florida, blending Puerto Rican and Italian cooking with the style of a New York deli. The chicken cutlet, meatball parm, sausage and peppers, and chicken or beef empanadas all come out fresh, made to order, straight from the window.

Customers keep coming back for the same reason: food made with care and direct attention on every visit. That is why the 34 Google reviews share the same word: fresh.
