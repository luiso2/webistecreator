About

Craftsmanship, with an eye for detail

John Copper Painting is a Tallahassee-based painting and finishing company built on careful prep work and clean execution. From full exterior repaints to refinishing a single front door, every job gets the same attention: tape lines that stay straight, surfaces prepped right, and a crew that cleans up after itself.

The result: a perfect 5.0-star rating across 11 Google reviews, free on-site estimates, and homeowners who call John Copper first the next time something needs a fresh coat, a stain, or a repair.
