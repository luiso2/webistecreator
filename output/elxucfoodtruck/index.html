The truck

One family,
the taste of El Salvador

El Xuc is a family-run Salvadoran food truck in Doral. Handmade pupusas, picadera el gordito, carne asada, Honduran baleadas and more, all made to order, from scratch, with real El Salvador recipes.

The reviews confirm it: "the best pupusa place I have found in Miami", everything made from scratch, with authentic flavor. A perfect 5.0 across 66 verified Google reviews.

Ordering is easy

How it works here

01

Call or walk up

Call the truck to ask what is on today, or just walk up: the full menu is always posted right on the truck.

02

Pick your plate

Pupusas, a sharing picadera, carne asada or baleadas: everything can be customized and is sold by the piece or by the plate.

03

Made fresh

Every pupusa and every plate is made by hand when you order it, nothing precooked a day ahead.

04

Dig in

Open-air tables right by the truck, to eat on the spot or take home.
