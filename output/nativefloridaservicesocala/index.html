About us

A family crew,
over 20 years of turf

Native Florida Services, LLC is a family owned company serving The Villages, Ocala and surrounding areas. Customers call and, most of the time, someone is at the house the same day or the next: no weeks-long wait for a simple job.

Over 20 years of experience and F.D.A.C.S. certified. The crew handles irrigation head donuts, drainage and pop-up drain installs, turf aeration and over-seeding, landscape lighting and palm tree installation, all with the same crew that answers the phone.
