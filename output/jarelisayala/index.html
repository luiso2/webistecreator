Your visit

How your visit unfolds

01

Consultation & skin check

Every visit starts with a quick consult: your goals, skin sensitivity and hair growth are checked before choosing the right wax or treatment for you.

02

Precision application

Using Berodin wax and clean, single-use tools, Jarelis works section by section for a thorough, low-pain result, whether it's a Brazilian, a brow shape or a facial.

03

Calm & soothe

Skin is soothed right after treatment; LED light therapy, a calming mask or a high-frequency treatment can be added to settle redness on sensitive skin.

04

Smooth finish

You leave with clean lines, defined brows or glowing skin, ready to live what Jarelis calls the smooth lifestyle.
