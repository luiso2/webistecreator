About us

A space built
to make you feel at ease

Blush Studios is a photography and production studio in White Plains. It is where portraits, headshots and family sessions happen, and the same space is rented out so other creators can run their own photo and video shoots.

Clients describe the experience as warm and patient, even in delicate sessions: from families with toddlers to headshots taken during a hard personal chapter. The goal is for the session to feel relaxed, and for that to show in the photos.
