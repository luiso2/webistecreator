The process, step by step

Here is how your visit unfolds

01

Consult & choose

A short talk on technique (gel, dip, dual form), length and shape before starting, so the visit matches what you have in mind.

02

Dry prep

Deep dry cleaning of nails and cuticles, Russian technique, with a focus on natural nail health.

03

Application & color

Gel, dip powder, builder gel or dual form applied with precision, with hand-painted nail art if you want it.

04

Finish & seal

Final glossy or matte top coat and a shape check. You leave with a manicure or pedicure built to last weeks.
