About us

Fresh batches
baked every morning

Mr. Donuts is a Kenneth City donut shop now under new ownership, baking everything from scratch every morning: glazed donuts, apple fritters, cinnamon rolls, croissants and kolaches, alongside breakfast sandwiches, burritos, boba tea and coffee.

Open daily from 5 AM, it has quickly built a loyal following for its huge portions, friendly service and a donut case that changes by the day, according to the shop's regulars.
