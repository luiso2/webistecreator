The salon

Seventeen years of
gentle hands

Doggie Paws Salon has spent 17 years bathing and grooming dogs on Lake Osborne Drive in Lake Worth. Owner Wanda Hernandez works alongside groomer Rosany Hernandez in a small, family-run salon where the team knows your dog by name.

Reviews say the same thing over and over: a dog that arrives nervous leaves excited to come back, careful haircuts and blowouts for poodles and doodles, and a team that sends photos mid-groom so you can approve the cut before pickup.

Your visit, step by step

Your visit, start to finish

01

Check-in & evaluation

Bring your dog to 1502 Lake Osborne Dr in Lake Worth. Wanda and Rosany take a few minutes to check the coat and read your dog's temperament before starting.

02

Bath & deep clean

A full bath and deep clean with careful, patient handling, the kind that turns a nervous dog into one that gets excited to come back.

03

Cut & style

A breed and coat-appropriate haircut and blowout, finished exactly the way you asked for it.

04

Finishing touches

Nail trim, ear cleaning and a free bandana. Ask for photos mid-groom and we will send them before you pick your dog up.
