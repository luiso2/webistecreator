About us

Arepas Doña Concha,
a Latin patio under the lights

Arepas Doña Concha is a Latin food truck with its own patio of tables, umbrellas and warm string lights in Medley, near North Miami, serving arepas, salchipapas and gourmet burgers every week.

The result: a 5.0-star rating on Google, with customers highlighting the service and calling the arepas some of the best they have tried.
