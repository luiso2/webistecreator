About us

The taste of Argentina,
right in your backyard

Tony Parrillada brings a mobile Argentinian grill to quinces, birthdays, weddings and corporate events around Hollywood and South Florida. Short rib, chorizo, morcilla, chicken and vegetables are cooked over open flame in front of guests, always finished with housemade chimichurri.

With 5.0 stars across 75 reviews, customers especially highlight the attention to detail and the chimichurri, described more than once as "a must try".
