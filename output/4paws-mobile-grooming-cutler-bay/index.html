About 4 Paws

One mobile groomer,
zero stress for your dog

4 Paws Mobile Dog Grooming is a mobile grooming service based in Cutler Bay, run by Kristin. Instead of loading your dog into the car and waiting in a salon, the full bath-and-haircut setup pulls right up to your driveway, all in one visit.

The service area covers Cutler Bay, Palmetto Bay, Kendall, Homestead, Coral Gables, Sunset and the surrounding South Miami-Dade neighborhoods. Every dog gets one-on-one attention, no crates and no other dogs barking in the background.
