How it works here

01

Book or walk in

Book your barber on Booksy or just walk in. With eight barbers on the floor, most days you are in the chair within minutes.

02

Talk it through

A fresh fade, a clean edge-up, or a full lineup with beard: you tell your barber what you want and they walk you through the options.

03

The cut

Clippers, precision lines, and the kind of attention to detail that has earned 1 Touch Barbershop more than 1,500 reviews.

04

Clean finish

Edge-up, hot towel, and you are out the door looking sharp. Book your next cut before you leave.
