About Us

One healer,
hands you can trust

Elsy Salgado was raised by a single mother from Colombia and Venezuela, an upbringing she credits with teaching her resilience and compassion. Today she is a Licensed Massage Therapist, a certified Yoga Instructor and a Traditional Chinese Medicine Physician, working out of her own holistic studio in Kendall.

She combines acupuncture, bodywork and movement to help manage pain and support overall health naturally, with a specialty in prenatal and postpartum massage that clients mention again and again.
