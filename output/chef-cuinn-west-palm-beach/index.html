About the chef

Cuinn Sealy,
chef de cuisine

Chef Cuinn is the catering and private chef service run by Cuinn Sealy, chef de cuisine based in West Palm Beach. He covers it all: intimate dinners at home, celebration brunches and catering for larger events, always working seafood, American, Italian and Caribbean fusion into the menu.

Clients keep saying the same thing in their Google reviews: professional, creative, and the kind of plating that gets people talking. Several describe working with him on their own catering projects, handling the budget and anything that comes up along the way.
