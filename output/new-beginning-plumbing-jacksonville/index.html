About us

A licensed contractor,
not a gamble

Dwayne Littlejohn runs New Beginning Plumbing Inc as a Florida State Certified General and Plumbing Contractor (license CGC1508110). Under Florida code, tub and shower valves, sinks, toilets and water heaters are required to be installed by a licensed contractor, and that license is right here, verified.

Clients describe Dwayne as punctual, communicative and careful with every detail, from protecting the floors to cleaning up after the job.
