About us

A veteran,
hands that know the work

Mobile Mike's Massage is a one-therapist, licensed mobile practice run by Michael Sciarratta, a military veteran who became a Licensed Massage Therapist (LMT) after his service. Since 2020 he's been bringing a professional table, oils and hot stones straight to homes across Pompano Beach and Broward County.

Every session starts the same way: he listens first. What hurts, what you want out of the hour, and he builds the pressure and technique around that answer, before setting up the table right in your own room.
