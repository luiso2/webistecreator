About LaLa's Bella

real moments

LaLa's Bella Photography is the work of Larisa Phillips, a Kissimmee photographer and mother of three. Her passion is babies, children and families: capturing the special bond between them, in relaxed sessions with no forced poses.

From maternity to courthouse weddings, cake smash and quinceañeras, every session is booked directly with her by call or text, no middlemen. The work is right there on Instagram, followed by 3,546 people.
