About us

One groomer,
one pet at a time

Bark and Wagging FL is a mobile grooming team serving Hollywood and nearby areas: dogs and cats, big breeds and small. Instead of dropping your pet off at a salon full of other animals, the groomer works one pet at a time inside a fully equipped van parked right outside your home.

Their Google reviewers mention groomers on the team like Oswaldo, Lina and Jose by name, and point to how calm their pets stay, from Ragdoll cats to Goldendoodles, thanks to a one-on-one, stress-free session.
