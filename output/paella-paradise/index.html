About us

Marlon brings the kitchen
to your party

Paella Paradise is a mobile caterer that cooks live in front of your guests: mixed and seafood paella in a giant pan, a whole roasted pig, oven-roasted pernil, and carefully built charcuterie tables. Nothing reheated in a tray: everything is prepared right at your event.

Marlon, the chef behind Paella Paradise, is mentioned again and again in reviews for his friendly service and attention to detail at every celebration.
