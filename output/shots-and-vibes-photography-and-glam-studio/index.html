About Us

A studio built
between sisters

Shots and Vibes started when Danita, behind the camera, began working alongside her sisters: one in makeup, one in hair, another in sales. What started as model shoots grew into a studio of their own in the heart of St. Petersburg, big enough for all four to work side by side.

Today the studio covers portraits, graduation, family, maternity and events, with hair and makeup available on site. The work speaks for itself: a perfect 5.0 rating on Google, session after session.
