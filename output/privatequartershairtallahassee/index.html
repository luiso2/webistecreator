About us

35 years of
hands you can trust

Brenda Moss, known to her clients as "Queen B", opened Private Quarters Hair Salon in Tallahassee's Frenchtown neighborhood around the idea of a spacious yet intimate salon. More than 35 years later, she is still a master stylist and instructor, and most of her clients have been with her for 20 years or more.

The team, with Ashley Moss, Chris, Shay and Sumayah, works silk press, relaxer, locs, weaves, cornrows, box plats and color. The salon has been recognized by Florida A&M University as Emerging Business of the Year and Outstanding Business of the Year.
